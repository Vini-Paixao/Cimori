Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a single professional monochrome logo concept comparison sheet for a new native Windows desktop app; 3 concepts, not a finished brand kit.
Product: independently branded successor to Isle and Mac Island & Dock; lives at top-center of the screen, gathers AI agent status, quick controls, music, agenda and notifications, light on resources, unobtrusive until needed. A new name is being chosen separately. Personality: quietly capable, precise, warm, compact, responsive.
Goal: a highly memorable SIMPLE symbol one recognizes instantly at 16 to 32 px. Must stand on its silhouette alone. Invent ORIGINAL marks, do not copy any known software logo. Avoid the generic blue-purple AI aesthetic.
Layout: horizontal 3-column presentation on warm white, generous negative space, equally optically sized large black symbols, thin quiet column separators. Small labels A, B, C only under respective symbols, no other text. Each symbol is flat solid black with true white negative space, max 2 or 3 geometric components, smooth exact curves, comfortably thick masses and open gaps.
A / 'Suspended crown': an abstract wide U-shaped cradle with rounded lower outer corners, thick walls, open upper center, and a SHORT horizontal rounded island floating ABOVE its opening, separate with a clear gap. Almost-square overall silhouette. The floating horizontal cap and receiving body imply the app's island at the top of the desktop and a calm place to gather activity. Not a shopping bag: the cap must be clearly detached and hover above the open body, never a handle. Refine into a unique coherent identity symbol.
B / 'Aperture': a compact bold C-like rounded-square mass with ONE decisively offset horizontal slit opening to the right, a negative-space horizon tucked into the upper third. Aim for an ownable abstract initial / aperture, not a stock C, G, chat bubble or power symbol. One continuous black mass, no decorative details.
C / 'Confluence': two soft but sturdy geometric pieces, diagonally offset, whose opposing inner concave cuts create a single short HORIZONTAL white island of negative space at their meeting point. Near-square silhouette, carefully balanced asymmetric arrangement; the two sources converge at a quiet central space. Avoid chain links, infinity loops, eyes, arrows, letter X, windows four squares, waves, audio bars, stars, robots, palm trees and literal islands.
No gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no textures, no outlines, no mockups, no glossy app tiles, no typography except the tiny A B C labels. Senior identity designer craft, exact vector-like geometry, strong clear silhouettes. This is an exploratory sheet to choose a direction.
